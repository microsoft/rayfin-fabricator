---
title: "Goals"
description: "Goals were removed from Fabricator. Use custom instructions to tell Copilot what every app must do, and the Advisor to check your app."
---

Goals are no longer in Fabricator. To tell Copilot what you want in every app you build, use [custom instructions](/docs/reference/settings#custom-instructions). To check your app before you publish it, use the [Advisor](/docs/ship/advisor).
